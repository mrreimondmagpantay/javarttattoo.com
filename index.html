What we do best

Five specialities, one standard: clean lines, smooth shading and designs that age well.

Fine-line tattoo

Delicate single-needle work: script, botanicals, small symbols and minimal designs with crisp, lasting lines.

Black & grey realism

Soft gradients and true-to-life shading in black and grey wash, from wildlife to statues and sleeves.

Portrait tattoo

Faces of loved ones, pets and icons, built from your photo with careful likeness and depth.

Pointillism

Dotwork shading and stipple texture for mandalas, geometric pieces and soft, grainy gradients.

Piercing

Ear, nose, lip, brow and more, done with sterile single-use needles and implant-grade jewellery.
